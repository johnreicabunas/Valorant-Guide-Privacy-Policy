# Valorant Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Valorant Guide is an unofficial Valorant companion guide app developed by John Rei Bobis Cabuñas. This Privacy Policy explains how Valorant Guide handles information when you use the app.

## Contact

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact:

John Rei Bobis Cabuñas  
johnreicabunas@gmail.com

## Information We Collect

Valorant Guide does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, precise location, payment information, Riot account information, gameplay account information, or advertising identifier.

The current version of Valorant Guide does not collect personal data. The app uses an offline guide snapshot and bundled app assets that are stored inside the app package.

Valorant Guide may keep simple app state locally on your device while you use the app, such as selected filters, search text, navigation state, or temporary screen selections. This information stays on your device and is used only to provide the app experience.

## How We Use Information

Valorant Guide uses bundled guide content and local app state only to provide guide functionality:

- To show searchable agents, roles, abilities, maps, weapons, and skins.
- To support filtering, detail pages, and quick navigation.
- To keep guide content available offline without requiring login.
- To provide a fast companion reference before or between matches.

## Data Sharing

Valorant Guide does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information.

The current version of Valorant Guide does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, account systems, purchases, or social login.

## Network Use

The shipped app does not require network access for guide browsing. Android builds include internet permission, but the current guide experience is powered by bundled local data and bundled image assets.

The developer data refresh script can download public Valorant API data and public game media during development. That script is not part of runtime guide browsing and is not used to collect user data.

## Device Permissions

The current version of Valorant Guide does not request sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or background location.

If a future version adds online guide refresh, analytics, ads, purchases, accounts, or other network features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before release.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Because Valorant Guide does not create user accounts or collect personal data, there is no account data to delete from our servers.

You can delete locally stored app data by clearing Valorant Guide's app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures for the app and its bundled content. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but Valorant Guide is designed to minimize privacy risk by avoiding collection of personal data in the current version.

## Children's Privacy

Valorant Guide is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Valorant Guide, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

## Changes To This Policy
